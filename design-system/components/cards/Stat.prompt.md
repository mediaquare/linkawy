Big orange number with a label — proof stats (270%), results, pricing figures.

```jsx
<Stat value="270%" label="Average organic sales growth" />
<Stat value="10+" label="Years of SEO" size="sm" />
<Stat value="$1,500" label="per month" gradient />   {/* exceptional card only */}
```

- Inside an orange highlight card the number turns white automatically (`--text-accent`).
- Numbers render in an LTR isolate so "%", "+" and "$" keep their position in Arabic.
