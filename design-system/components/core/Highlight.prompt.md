Inline emphasis for headings: `Highlight` draws the orange marker behind a phrase; `Accent` turns key words orange.

```jsx
<h2>SEO is the <Highlight>last step</Highlight> we take</h2>
<h1>The SEO agency for <Accent>e-commerce stores</Accent> and companies</h1>
```

- One highlighted phrase per heading. Don't combine Highlight + Accent on the same words.
- Works the same in Arabic; the mark follows the text direction.
