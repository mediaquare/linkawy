The page-section wrapper: background, 112/80px vertical rhythm and the 1200px container.

```jsx
<Section bg="dark"><SectionHeading title="…" /></Section>
<Section bg="cream" size="sm">…</Section>
<Section bg="dark" beforeSunset>…</Section>
```

- Sections are always straight (radius 0). Never wrap section content in a big rounded panel.
- Never put two cream sections back to back. One dark = #0A0A0A for every dark section.
- Homepage order: dark · white · cream · (sunset) · dark · dark · white · cream · white · cream · white · white · cream-warm · dark · Footer E.
